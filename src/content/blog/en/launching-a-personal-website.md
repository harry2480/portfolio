If all goes well, I'll no longer be a student come March, which is a little sad.

My previous phone was an iPhone 14, so this was a jump of three generations. I upgraded because my birthday was coming up, I wanted to get rid of Lightning cables at home, and my old camera was weak, so I wanted to try a Pro model at least once.

In the end, I'm glad I upgraded.
The zoom is excellent. It's been a big help at concerts and when I end up in the back of a classroom and can't see the blackboard or monitor well. That said, it's showing behavior that looks like an initial defect, so I plan to use it a bit longer, keep some logs, and then take it to the Apple Store.

## Looking Ahead to 2026

Looking back, I feel this has been a good year.

Next year, I first need to graduate, and then start my job.

I've received a job offer in solution sales, so I plan to work in sales for a while. The company's stance is "you don't have to stay too long," so my plan is to give it my all for about 5 to 10 years, and if I'm not satisfied, consider changing jobs. Career paths are something I've been thinking about quite a lot lately.

I'd also like to keep writing articles on a whim as a steady form of output. I plan to keep posting the various things I think about and other miscellaneous topics.
